=== what was built ===
* Protocol: repl_sync_wal takes optional "<expected_epoch> <max_batches> <max_bytes>". The source answers with
  "EPOCH <source_epoch> <latest_lsn>" BEFORE any batch, refuses a follower of another history with
  epoch_mismatch, refuses to serve at all when it cannot identify its history (generations_unavailable), and ends
  the slice with MORE when further updates are waiting. A source that predates the tokens ignores them and sends
  no EPOCH line, and the follower then refuses to follow it — fail closed.
* Master memory: get_updates_since now STOPS READING at the caller's bounds instead of materialising a far-behind
  reader's whole backlog (design §4, condition 7); the reconstruction path still asks for an unbounded read.
* Follow applies through the COMMON APPLY RULE (apply_wal_batch), never verbatim. The reconstruction path, where
  the node takes no forwarded writes, keeps its verbatim apply.
* handler_wal_follower: resumes from the applied position, polls on its own schedule so a replica catches up after
  a blip WITHOUT any new write arriving, reconnects with bounded backoff, and hands the node to the rebuild path
  ONLY on a position that can never be satisfied again.
* stats gains the follower record as ONE snapshot: source, source epoch, state
  (idle/initial_sync/following/disconnected/needs_rebuild/error), last reason, applied position, the source's
  position AND the time it was observed, and the time the position last advanced. Exposed as repl_follow_*,
  repl_applied_lsn, repl_source_lsn, repl_source_lsn_observed_at, repl_last_progress_at.

=== NOT executed / still open ===
* The follower is NOT started by any lifecycle yet: nothing constructs handler_wal_follower in cluster's role
  transitions, so continuous replication does not run in a real node. That wiring, and the operator-side
  eligibility and ledger coordination, are SAF-10c.
* repl-identity-forward is still OFF by default; no E2E exercises either path yet (SAF-10d).
* Lock-hold time and writer starvation bounds (design §3.8, T17): still deferred by agreement.
* The follow transport has only unit-level coverage of its decision table; the wire handshake itself (EPOCH line,
  MORE, epoch_mismatch) is exercised only through code review so far.
